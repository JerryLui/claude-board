# 124. The home page is Missions, in columns by state, under one top bar

2026-10-28, relates to 30, 46, 87, 98, 123

**Context:** missions (123) needed a page, and `/` was the boards list, capped at 900px while a board page runs to 2560px. Three layouts were mocked: a needs-you-first list, a card per mission carrying its phase track, and columns by state; and the first cut kept the boards list below the missions on one page. **Decision:** `/` is the Missions view, five columns by derived state (Needs you, Working, Waiting, Stalled, Landed), a queued mission shown under Waiting with a Queued tag. The boards list with its filter moves to `/boards`, and a `/?q=` link from before redirects there. Both views share one top bar, the board page's own header in the board page's shell: the window's width up to 2560px, the 30px mark and 20px title, the timer, Comment and theme controls, and two tabs, Missions and Boards, the Boards tab counting rounds waiting. Switching tabs moves nothing in the bar, and the boards list fills its width. Needs you takes the amber every *waiting on you* surface spends (30). Comment is on in the bar while a card is shown, since a note on a card reaches its session through the Master Chief (46, 98). **Consequences:** an old bookmark to `/` opens Missions, and a machine that never runs a mission opens on an empty state; the menu bar's overflow row opens `/boards`; the home views scroll their root always (`overflow-y: scroll`), so a short view and a long one keep one width; the page structure and the rejected layouts are argued in DESIGN.md, "Entry 124".
