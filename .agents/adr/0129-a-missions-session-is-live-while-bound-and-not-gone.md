# 129. A mission's session is live while bound and not gone

2026-10-28, relates to 123, 125; amended by 140

**Context:** a mission takes one live session at a time, and the launch check needs a reading of live that survives a long silent turn, or a director idle while its owners run, yet frees a mission whose start failed or whose work ended. Rejected: silence alone freeing the mission, which starts a successor beside a session still running. **Decision:** a session holds its mission while it is bound (a pid or a session id) or reserved at launch, its mission has not recorded its end, and it is not gone. Gone is a bound pid that no longer exists, or a reservation no process binds within 5 minutes. A working session silent 15 minutes reads stalled, and keeps its mission and its slot while its pid lives; silence frees only a session bound without a pid. A mission records its session's end by landing, or by a grill recording Fire, and an ended session never reads stalled. A launch ends the session it replaces, and that session's pid and id may not bind its successor; a bind naming another pid, or another id when the bound session has no pid, while the bound session holds is a 409, and the bound pid under a new id is its heartbeat, taking the id. A launch onto a live session is a 409 naming it, with `silent: true` when it reads stalled by silence; past 6 working or 12 alive sessions a launch queues instead, holding no session until it launches. **Consequences:** a hung process with a live pid holds its mission until something ends it, and the 409's `silent` is how a launcher tells a hang from a busy session; a stalled session still counts as working toward the cap, and one waiting on the person counts as alive but not working.
