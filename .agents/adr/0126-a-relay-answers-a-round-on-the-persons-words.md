# 126. A relay answers a round on the person's words, marked relayed

2026-10-28, relates to 114, 115, 125

**Context:** a person who types "yes, merge it" to the Master Chief means it for a session waiting on a merge round. Carried as a message between sessions, a yes is indistinguishable from one any session on the shared bus could make up, and a broad "merge everything green" forged that way would merge every PR. **Decision:** the Master Chief answers the round itself, through `POST /api/board/:id/relay { round, answers, words }`: the submit route's own path, so the round is sent, pushed to its tabs and wakes a waiting `ask`, and the round records `relayed: { words, at }`, carried by every packet and `read` of it and said in a `Relayed:` line on the text channel. `words`, the person's own, verbatim, is required and at most 1 KB. The route takes the secret only, never the cookie. A sent round is a 409, and so is an abandoned one, which a Send would still take (114): nobody waits on a yes there. **Consequences:** a session acts on a yes only from its own round, and can tell a relayed answer from a Send on the page; the residual: any process of the same user holding the secret could post a relay, as it could already post a submit, so `relayed` proves which path answered, not that the person typed the words.
