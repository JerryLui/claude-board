# 120. One Pull-out in the corner of every rendered block

2026-10-12 · relates to 22, 110

**Context:** the Lens was reached by an "expand" pill in a top-level block's label row or a context diagram's tools row, and a stage inside a question had none at all; the alternatives were adding the pill to that one gap or moving stages alone. **Decision:** every stage and every diagram, wherever it sits, carries one always-visible Pull-out in its frame's top-right corner, the only way into the Lens, and the label-row and tools-row pills go. **Consequences:** the control overlaps the artifact's own top-right corner; a page board and a block that failed to render carry none.
