# 84. One signal, one dimension, in the status glyph

2026-08-10 · narrows 80; relates to 83; narrowed by 94; narrowed by 103

**Context:** the glyph had run out of room. Fill meant "long break" and was also wanted for progress; the two bars meant "break" and were also the obvious drawing of "paused"; opacity meant both "paused" and "the daemon has gone quiet". Every attempt to add a state collided with one already there, and a filled interior occluded whatever mark sat in the middle. **Decision:** every signal gets exactly one dimension and no other. The tomato silhouette — circle, stem and leaves — is drawn in every state, so the break glyph gains the stem it deliberately lacked. Time remaining is a thin ring just inside the outline, draining clockwise, never a fill. Resting is one flat bar in the centre. Paused is two vertical bars in the centre and no ring at all, on every phase alike. Overall opacity means only that the daemon has stopped answering. **Consequences:** short and long break become one glyph and the long break's filled disc is retired, that distinction having no dimension left to spend; a paused Timer no longer says which phase it is paused in, which is accepted because a paused Timer has nothing to be late for and the Popover is one click away; and ADR 80's own enumeration of the four states no longer describes what ships, though its decision — a template image, shape and weight only, never hue — is exactly what forced this one.
