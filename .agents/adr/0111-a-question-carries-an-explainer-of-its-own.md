# 111. A question carries an explainer of its own

2026-09-03 · relates to 26, 110

**Context:** the grill's one or two sentences ahead of the options had no home but `context`, which lays out beside the options, so a question's own explanation stacked as a labelled card next to what it explained. **Decision:** a question block may carry `explainer`, markdown by value, rendered as plain prose under the prompt on the card's full row, above both the options and the context panel; `context` is left for what the reviewer looks up. **Consequences:** a field on the question block, named in PROTOCOL.md, the manual and every caller that grills; a question without one renders as before.
