# 132. Any block may carry card fields

2026-10-29, relates to 133

**Context:** an agent showing evidence for a change (a criterion's title, a verdict, one plain sentence, the detail behind it) drew it as a hand-built html stage, apart from the board's own blocks, so the two looks drifted; giving every block the evidence card's look (133) needed a way for a block to say its title and status. Rejected: keeping that card a hand-drawn stage, which drifts from the board by construction. **Decision:** any block may carry optional card fields wherever it sits (top level, a compare side, a question's `context`, a rendered variant's `block`): `title`, plain text of 1 to 200 characters; `status`, `{ label, tone }` with a label of 1 to 40 characters and a tone of `good`, `warn`, `bad` or `neutral`; `summary`, markdown of 1 to 600 characters; and `details`, markdown under the by-value cap, folded closed, with an optional `detailsLabel` of 1 to 60 characters allowed only beside `details`. A `question` refuses `title`, `status` and `summary`, since its prompt, answer state and explainer fill those, and takes `details` and `detailsLabel`. Each field is absent when not sent, never empty; a wrong type or length is a 400 naming the field; `summary` and `details` are stored as markdown with their HTML rendered at post time, as a question's explainer is. **Consequences:** a board stored before carries none of them and renders as it did, in the titled-card look; ids inside a summary's or details' HTML may repeat across cards, as an explainer's already do.
