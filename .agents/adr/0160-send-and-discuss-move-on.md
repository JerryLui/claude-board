# 160. Send and Discuss move on: to the board's next waiting round, else home

2026-11-25, amends 134; relates to 42, 55, 91, 93, 114

**Context:** after a Send or a Discuss went out the page stayed on the round it had just closed, and on a phone the PM tapped the logo to get back to Missions after every round. Leaving is not free: answers, queued comments and pasted images live only in the open page (134 keeps another open round's typing there, with no draft saved anywhere), and a board left with a round still waiting and nobody watching raises the stranded banner (55). **Decision:** on every screen, the Mac included, once the PM's own press of Send or Discuss succeeds its status ("Sent." or "Handed over to chat.") shows for a second; then the page flips to the oldest other round on the board that still waits: one that owes an answer, is still awaited, or holds comments its own Send would post or a comment typed or pasted and not yet added, saying "Round 9 still waits" in that round's send bar; the phone pill's dot (42) lights on the same rule, so Send never flips to a round that showed none; and with none it goes where the Home mark goes (Missions or Boards, the last used). A Send that failed, a 409 (sent by another tab) and the deadline's own flush of a page round's comments stay on the round; a round opened from disk never moves. Rejected: moving on on phones only, the Mac tab staying on the round; going home past a waiting round; going home always. **Consequences:** on a Mac the board's next round arrives as a banner after its grace (55, 91), not in the open tab; each banner click opens a new tab, since no tab shows the board any more (93), so home tabs pile up; with no board tab open a new board's first round opens a tab in front again (91; the home page is no watcher); at banner level off nothing announces it; Discuss leaves the round being discussed, one click away on Boards.
