# 156. The pill reads `open` on an unsent round whose Send works

2026-11-16, narrows 89; relates to 44, 114

**Context:** on an ordinary board a round nobody is waiting on kept the pill's word `read-only` (89) above widgets that take answers and a Send that stores them (114). Only the hover text said so, and a reader who went by the word left the round unanswered. Rejected: keeping `read-only` and leaving it to the hover text, which is the reading that left those rounds unanswered. **Decision:** on an ordinary board an unsent round's pill reads `open` wherever Send works on it: the newest unsent round, and an older one that still asks a question. The word goes by the send bar's own rule, never a copy of it: the page hands `pageBoardPillMeta` what its send bar goes by (`currentPageSendable`), the first paint what its own does (`hasOpenRound`). Every other closed round keeps `read-only`: an older unsent round that asks nothing, a full-page round (44), and every round of a board opened from disk. A Submitted round keeps `submitted`, an awaited one its countdown, and no hover text changes. **Consequences:** the served page is also the file an archive opens, and cannot know which it will be, so an archive's first paint says `open` and the page's script puts `read-only` back as it loads. Never-Awaited, Lapsed and Abandoned rounds still share one word (89, 114): the pill tells a reader whether an answer can be sent from here, not how the wait ended.
