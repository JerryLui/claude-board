# 155. A reopened round holds its mission's card at needs you behind a later round

2026-11-16, amends 134, 151; relates to 150

**Context:** a mission's card read needs you from each session's newest round only (134, 150), and a round a hand-over reopened is its new session's (151), so that session's next round, asking nothing, took the card off needs you while the question the mission was handed still waited: it showed under Waiting on you on the Boards page alone, and the Missions view, where the PM looks for what waits on them, said nothing did. Rejected: needs you from any round that still wants an answer, which holds a card for good on every older round whose wait lapsed, since such a round stays `open`, the Lapsed page 134 took off the card, and would draw a Fire from a page long passed. **Decision:** a linked board holds its mission at needs you when a session's newest round on it wants an answer, as before, or when a round carrying `reopenedAt` does (`open`, and asking a question or minted with a deadline), whatever rounds its session posted since. The card's Fire and Merge come from each session's newest round only, as before. **Consequences:** the card clears when the last such round is sent or closed by number (`declined`), the two ends 151 gives a reopened round; one whose new wait lapsed keeps the card until then, as a newest round's lapsed wait does, the rule reading nothing off the clock; an older round never reopened stays off the card; a Fire or Merge question in a reopened round behind a later one is answered from its page on the board, not from the card; a session working on while a reopened round is owed, behind a later round or still its newest, keeps its working slot of the launch caps (149): only a needs you held by a session's newest round that no hand-over reopened frees one, that session waiting on the PM, and the view carries `asking` to tell the two apart; a tab card is unchanged, reading every round of its board already.
