# 151. A hand-over reopens the last session's unanswered rounds in place

2026-11-15, amends 150; relates to 69, 114, 145, 146

**Context:** a round its session left unanswered stayed closed for good: a boundary abandoned it (69) or its wait lapsed, and a takeover handed over only rounds still `open`, without a wait (150). The PM's late Send was stored (114) and reached a session only through `read`, so the question a mission still owed an answer to sat read-only while a new session worked it, and the taker's first `fresh` ask closed even the rounds it had been handed. Rejected: asking the question again as a new round, which loses the PM's picks, notes and comments in the open tab and leaves two rounds for one question; restarting only timed-out rounds, which leaves the ones a `/clear` closed. **Decision:** when a session binds in place of another (a join after the bound one went or was replaced, a takeover's leave, and the bound process joining under a new id after a `/clear`), every round the previous session left unsent on the boards the mission links is handed to it (`session` rewritten, `handedFrom` the giver's id), and each that asks for an answer (minted with a deadline) is reopened in place: `open`, `awaited`, a deadline one wait from now, `reopenedAt` stamped, `abandonedAt` dropped, the round's number, blocks and comments untouched. Only the previous session's rounds: an earlier session's stay closed, so a long mission's old rounds do not all come back. The join answers `reopened`, `[{ board, round, title, url, awaitDeadline }]`, on every join while such a round waits, and `GET /api/binding` carries the list the latest join or takeover recorded, which is how a takeover's taker learns it. The PM's Send reaches the new session as any round of its own does: its next packet, `read`, or the answer watch (146), whose command the shim's join adds as `watch`. A `fresh` post spares a reopened round, and a later question never amends one; the session closes one it does not want by number (`POST /api/board/:id/abandon` with `round`, the shim's `mission close`), which stamps `declined`, and no later hand-over reopens a declined round. **Consequences:** Abandoned and Lapsed are no longer final for a mission's round, so the stranded rule's mark keeps a round announced once from a second banner; a reopened round nobody answers lapses again after its wait and reopens at the next hand-over; a tab already open shows the new countdown on a lapsed round at once and on an abandoned one after a reload, its Send live throughout (114); the same-process `/clear` case rests on the round's recorded session id, so a round posted before the session watcher listed its session is not reopened; the mission's card reads needs you from each session's newest round only (150), so a reopened round behind its new session's later round shows under Waiting on you on the Boards page alone.
