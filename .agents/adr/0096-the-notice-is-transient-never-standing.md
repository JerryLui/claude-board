# 96. The Notice is transient, never standing

2026-08-11 · relates to 28

**Context:** With Comment mode on, a click on a question option is swallowed by the same guard
that gates anchoring, and nothing on the page says so — a reviewer reads it as a broken option.
A per-option hint answering this once existed and was deleted for repeating in the place
vertical space is scarcest, so the obvious repair is the one already rejected.

**Decision:** A single transient Notice, raised by the blocked click itself, floating at the
round footer and fading after 5s; a second blocked click resets its timer rather than adding a
copy. Rejected: a standing strip under the header, which spends vertical space on every round
whether or not anyone is confused, and any cue attached to the options themselves.

**Consequences:** A reviewer who looks away misses it, since nothing persists after the fade —
accepted because the toggle remains visible chrome saying the mode is on.
