# 123. The board keeps missions beside its rounds

2026-10-28, relates to 5, 71, 115; amended by 130; narrowed by 134

**Context:** a person running a dozen agent sessions at once asked each for its status by hand, and was the only link between them; the board already saw every session's rounds but held nothing about the work around them. The alternative on the table kept status outside the board: files in the agent's own config, with the Master Chief re-posting an overview board whenever one changed, a round per change and never live. **Decision:** claude-board holds a Mission per piece of work, one record under `missions/<key>.json` across its grill, direct and landing sessions, keyed by a short name such as a spec's descriptor. Any agent writes it, Claude, Codex or OpenCode alike, through the shim's `mission` tool, and a hook writes it over `POST /api/mission/:key` with the local secret; the writable fields are a whitelist, each typed and capped, holding work names, phases, links and closing summaries. A board posted with `mission: <key>` (the shim sends it from `CLAUDE_MISSION`) is linked to that mission from the post's own reply. The Missions view and the Master Chief read the same records, live, through `GET /api/missions` and the daemon-wide stream's `missions` event. The column a mission shows in is derived on every read from its sessions and boards, never stored. **Consequences:** the board's pitch widens from a decision surface to decisions plus status; a mission never carries case content, a rule the whitelist can bound but not enforce, so it rests on the writing agent as entry 5 leaves every caller's prose to its caller; a landed mission leaves the default list a day after it lands, and its record goes only through the hand-fired prune, 30 days after `retiredAt` (71); a reader re-reads on a timer as well as on the stream, since a stall changes no byte on disk (128).
