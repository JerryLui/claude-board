# 152. The top strip takes clicks on its controls only, holds Download round on a full-page round, and leaves for a mock's full-size view

2026-11-15, amends 40; relates to 33, 59, 120

**Context:** the strip's box spans the column, and the whole window over a full-page mock, around a wash or a pill drawn far smaller (40), and that box took every click under it: an option lying under the header, and each button of a full-size view a mock opens over itself (a modal dialog inside its stage, which runs to the frame's top edge and keeps its own bar there, exactly where the strip floats). The box cannot shrink to what is drawn: 40 holds it at one size so condensing never reflows the page. A round drawn full page (33) also has no chip line, so it had nowhere to offer Download round, and the strip is the one piece of board chrome that stays on screen once the artifact scrolls. Rejected: teaching each mock template to keep its full-size view's bar clear of the band, which leaves every mock not built from one broken (59: the board clears its own band, the artifact does not). **Decision:** three rules for the strip of a board page, the home page's top bar untouched. It takes a click on its two control groups only: the strip's own box is `pointer-events: none`, each child box `auto`, stated by container so a control added to either group takes clicks with no rule of its own. Its controls are mark, comment toggle, Download round, theme, then the state label, the same order expanded, condensed and narrow; once the artifact scrolls under the condensed bar, Download round is its icon alone, named Download round. Download round is in every board's strip, shown only while the round in view is drawn full page, where it saves that round, and hidden wherever a round has its own on a chip line and in a read-only archive. And it leaves while the mock on screen has a full-size view open in its band: the stage, which alone can see its own dialogs, posts `modal` with `open` true when a modal dialog of its reaches above the band's lower edge and false when the last one has left, only ever on a page board; the board marks the stage's block on a real boolean and hides the strip (`visibility: hidden`, so it keeps its box and the band the stage pads for holds still) while the round in view holds a marked block. A stage that announces itself again clears the mark. **Consequences:** a click on the strip's empty part reaches the page under it, so nothing may rely on the strip to shield what lies below; 40's control order gains a fourth control, and a new strip control needs no click rule; a dialog that stays clear of the band keeps the strip. Known limit: `modal` is stage-authored, so any script in a mock can post `open: true` with no dialog open, and the strip then stays hidden while that round is on screen, with no control of the board's to bring it back short of flipping to another round. The loss is the strip's own controls (the way home, comment mode, theme, Download round) and nothing else: a hidden strip takes no clicks, and Send and the pager sit outside it, so a mock cannot decide anything this way.
