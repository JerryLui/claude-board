# 136. From 1440px every card on a board page is one width, up to 1700px

2026-10-29, narrows 121, 122; relates to 26, 110, 120

**Context:** the board's column grew to the window's width, up to 2560px, in the change that brought ADRs 121 and 122, so a mock could render at a screen's size; every card then spanned the window while its text kept a 72-character measure, so at 2560px text and questions held a card's left third, a question's context panel ran to the right edge mostly empty, diagrams sat small in big panels, and a question's note (635px) lined up with nothing above it (options at 523px). The first pick was cards that fit their content; on its mock the person found the widths differed widely and asked for one width, as the comparison had, and the next round set it. Rejected: cards that fit their content (widths varied widely), every card the window's width (short text and narrow tables left cards half empty), and one centred column (text no longer lines up with the title). **Decision:** from a 1440px window every card on a board page, pictures included, is the column's width up to 1700px on the header's left edge, and the window beyond it is plain page; the header keeps the shell's width up to 2560px, and a page board still fills the window. Long open text flows into up to two columns of reading width (72ch); short text, a folded preview, and text holding a table, code or a diagram stay one column. A question's options take a third of the card, never under 523px, with its note and Defer row under them at that width and its context the other two thirds, tables at their natural width; a rendered-variant question, or one carrying a stage in its context, keeps one full-width column (26) and its note at reading width. Compare sides take half; a narrow diagram is centred; a picture opens full size from its corner Pull-out (120). Under 1440px nothing changes. **Consequences:** on a window wider than about 1760px a top-level mock renders about 1650px wide, its card less the card's padding, rather than near the window's width; a long title stays at reading width; the board header and its wash still span the window, its title's edge aligned with the cards.
