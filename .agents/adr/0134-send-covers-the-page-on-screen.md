# 134. Send covers the page on screen, and needs you reads a board's newest asking round

2026-10-29, narrows 123, 125; relates to 42, 114

**Context:** a board can hold more than one unsent round, each its own page (42). With the newest page fully answered, Send counted every open page, stopped on a blank in an older, hidden page and jumped there, while a submit names one round, so what was typed on the other page was dropped without a word; and a mission read needs you from any linked round still asking, so a stale older page held it there. Rejected: one Send for every open page, closing them all, which changes the daemon and the public protocol and needs guards for display pages, Fire, Merge and relays on older pages, and the answer watch. **Decision:** Send's check, its questions-left count, its unanswered ask and the answers it posts read only the page on screen, and its submit names that page's round; the daemon already takes a submit naming any unsent round (a sent one is a 409, and an abandoned one a late Send, 114), so neither it nor the protocol changes. Another open page keeps what was typed on it and its pager dot, and sends from its own page; a page flip disarms an armed Send. A mission's needs you, and its card's Fire and Merge, come only from each linked board's newest round, while that round asks something. **Consequences:** two open asking pages take two Sends; Send posts the comments on its own page's blocks, and the newest open page's Send also those on pages that ask nothing, which have no Send of their own, while the rest stay queued for their own page's Send; the send bar's countdown shows the newest open page; an older page left open shows only on the board's pager, never on the mission's card.
