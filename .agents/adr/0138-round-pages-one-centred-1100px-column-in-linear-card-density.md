# 138. Round pages are one centred 1100px column, in Linear's card density

2026-10-29, replaces 136; narrows 121, 122; relates to 26, 110, 120, 133, 137

**Context:** 136 held every card on a board page to the column's width up to 1700px on the header's left edge, so on a wide window the page sat against its left side with long, loose cards, while the home page (137) had moved to Linear's list density; the two read as different products. Recommended was a 760px text column with tables, pictures and compares widening to 1360px on the same centre (lines of about 85 characters, bigger mocks). The person chose one width for every card instead, accepting lines of about 130 characters at 13px and smaller mocks, which reverses 136's rejection of a centred column. **Decision:** from a 1440px window every card on a board page, the board's title and its meta line sit in one 1100px column (`--round-column`), centred with `margin-inline: auto`; the header keeps the shell's width so its mark, buttons, wash and condensed pill stay put, and only its title block moves onto the column; the home page's top bar shares the header's classes and keeps its title beside the mark. Prose carries no line-length cap from 1440px; long open text past about ten lines flows into two columns; a question's options take the card's left half, never under 523px, with a 20px gap and its context in the right half, and a question whose context or options hold a stage stacks at full width (26). Under 1440px the layout is as before. At every width a round page's cards take Linear's density, the home page's (137): 13px card text, 14 by 16 padding, 8px corners (`--r-card`) and 8px between cards, card titles 13px semibold and nested titles 12.5px, the board title 20px semibold, options 13px with 12px descriptions, markdown headings 16, 14.5 and 13px. **Consequences:** at 1440px the header's buttons narrow the title box to about 1011px, its left edge still on the column; a top-level mock renders at about 1070px however wide the window; a page board keeps no column and still fills the window.
