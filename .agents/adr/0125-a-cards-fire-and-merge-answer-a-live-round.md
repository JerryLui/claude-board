# 125. A card's Fire and Merge answer a live round; Stop is an order

2026-10-28, relates to 46, 114, 123, 126; narrowed by 134, 142

**Context:** the answers a mission most often waits on are a grill's Fire and a landing's merge, each one click deep in its round; a card could offer them, and a card could ask a session to stop. Rejected: read-only cards linking into rounds, and a button that writes its own state on the mission, a second path to a yes beside the round. **Decision:** a `single` question may carry `action: 'fire'` or `'merge'`, its yes as option 0. While that question's round still waits on an answer, on a board linked to the mission, the view lists it in `actions` and the card shows a Fire or Merge button; a click is that round's normal submit with that one question set to option 0, exactly a Send with that pick. A round that sends a `fire` question answered yes, from the card, its page or a relay, records `fire: { at, by }` on the mission in that same submit (`by: 'card'` when the card's body says `via: 'card'`), so the board records the Fire itself and it never waits on the grill session that asked being alive to copy it. Stop, a circle-slash icon shown while the mission's session is live and confirmed once (a Cmd-click skips the question), and a comment on a card each append an order (`POST /api/mission/:key/order`, the one mission write the browser's cookie may make); the Master Chief relays it to the session and acks it, and the board signals no process. **Consequences:** a button shows only while the round asks for it, so a sent or abandoned round offers none, though an abandoned one still takes a late Send on its own page (114), while a round whose wait lapsed unanswered still offers it; every gate a merge carries beyond the round stays with the agent; Stop never kills: the session finishes its step, writes its handoff and ends, and an order nobody relays stays pending on the card.
