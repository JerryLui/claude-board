# 94. The ring is work's alone

2026-08-11 · narrows 84; relates to 83

**Context:** ADR 84 gave time remaining one dimension — a thin ring just inside the outline — and said nothing about which phase draws it, so every running interval did. Measured, the ring's outer edge lands 0.15 units inside the outline's inner edge, about a tenth of a point at the shipped scale: two strokes that near read as one thick stroke rather than as a ring inside a circle. A running break therefore drew the outline, a near-touching arc and the rest bar, and came out as the densest glyph in the vocabulary during the one interval nobody is meant to be watching the menu bar. **Decision:** only a running work interval draws the ring. A break, short and long alike, is the silhouette and the rest bar; paused, on every phase, stays the silhouette and the two bars. The ring is thereby a second reading of the phase as well as of the clock — a ring means work — and a break's glyph becomes byte-for-byte the drawing `REST_ICON` already ships on the index page. **Consequences:** the glyph no longer says how much of a break is left, which the countdown digits beside it still do and the Popover says in words; `fraction` is still derived for every running state, drawn or not, so the probe keeps reporting it and the phase-to-duration mapping keeps its check; and the ring and a centre mark can no longer appear together, though the ring still keeps clear of the centre, the fill that occluded it being what 84 retired.
