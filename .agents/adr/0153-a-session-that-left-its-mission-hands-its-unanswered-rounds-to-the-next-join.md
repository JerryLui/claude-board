# 153. A session that left its mission hands its unanswered rounds to the next join

2026-11-16, amends 151; relates to 150

**Context:** a hand-over reopened the rounds of a session that went, was replaced or was taken over, and named a session that left its mission on purpose (`/mission off`, no takeover pending) no giver (151): the rounds it left unanswered stayed closed, off Waiting on you, a late Send reaching a session only through `read`, though the mission still owed those answers and a takeover already moves a live session's rounds (150). Why they do not stay closed: DESIGN.md, "Entry 153". **Decision:** a session that left with no takeover pending gives its rounds as a takeover's giver does, at the next join, one it still waits on included. The record that takes its place, a session bound or a launch's reservation or queue, carries it as `left`, `{ pid?, procStart?, id? }`, never as `ended`: it stays a tab, may join again and is no session of the mission's. A join that binds hands its rounds over on every board the mission links and then drops `left`; while a board fails to read or to be handed over the record keeps it, and the next join hands over again. The join's answer never carries `left`. Joining again itself under the id it left with, it is handed nothing and the record drops it; under a new id after a `/clear`, its rounds as any other session, save where a round on the mission's boards is recorded under the id it joins with: it asked that round after a `/clear` while bound and left under the new id, so only the rounds recorded under the id the mission bound it with are handed, and the round it asked since stays as it was. Only the session before the one that joins gives: a session bound by a heartbeat alone is handed the leaver's rounds at its join, and when it goes, leaves or is taken over before one, they stay the leaver's. **Consequences:** a leaver still waiting on its question loses the round to the session that joins, its answer watch answered as a takeover giver's is; a join whose hand-over failed on a board still answers 200, and the round on that board waits for that session's next join; a leaver back under a new id whose rounds record no session id (posted before the session watcher listed it) is handed them by its process, and beside a round recorded under its new id they stay closed, as the same-process `/clear` case leaves them (151).
