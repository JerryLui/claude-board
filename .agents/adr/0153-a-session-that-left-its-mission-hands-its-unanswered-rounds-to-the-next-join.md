# 153. A session that left its mission hands its unanswered rounds to the next join

2026-11-16, amends 151; relates to 150

**Context:** a hand-over reopened the rounds of a session that went, was replaced or was taken over, and named a session that left its mission on purpose (`/mission off`, no takeover pending) no giver (151): the rounds it left unanswered stayed closed, off Waiting on you, a late Send reaching a session only through `read`, though the mission still owed those answers and a takeover already moves a live session's rounds (150). Why they do not stay closed: DESIGN.md, "Entry 153". **Decision:** a session that left with no takeover pending gives its rounds as a takeover's giver does, at the next join, one it still waits on included. The record that takes its place, a session bound or a launch's reservation or queue, carries it in `left`, a list of `{ pid?, procStart?, id? }`, never as `ended`: it stays a tab, may join again and is no session of the mission's. A join that binds hands its rounds over on every board the mission links and then drops `left`; while a board fails to read or to be handed over the record keeps it, and the next join hands over again. The join's answer never carries `left`. Joining again itself under the id it left with, it is handed nothing and the record drops it; under a new id after a `/clear`, its rounds as any other session, by its process, save a round recorded under the id it joins with: no hand-over gives a session a round recorded under its own id, so a round it asked after a `/clear` while bound, or was handed at a join that failed on another board, stays as it was. `left` names every session that left since the last join that was handed every board, and each gives: a session bound by a heartbeat alone is handed the leaver's rounds at its join, and when it goes or leaves before one, the record that takes its place carries the leaver on, beside that session itself, to the next session that joins; a takeover of it hands them to the taker at the leave that binds it. A session whose join was handed them ends the line. Why they do not stay the leaver's, and why the record holds no joined stamp: DESIGN.md, "Entry 153". **Consequences:** a leaver still waiting on its question loses the round to the session that joins, its answer watch answered as a takeover giver's is; a join whose hand-over failed on a board still answers 200, and the round on that board waits for the next join, that session's or the one after it; a leaver back under a new id is handed by its process every round that records no session id (posted before the session watcher listed it), one it asked after the `/clear` among them, which nothing tells apart; a mission whose sessions leave one after another with no join between goes on naming each in `left`, once.
