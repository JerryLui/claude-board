# 110. A question's context is one panel, and long prose folds

2026-09-03 · widens 26; relates to 28, 111

**Context:** a stage-free question rendered every context item as its own card with a kind label beside the options, so a grill's explainer, diagram and referenced section read as a pile of panels, and a long item ran a screen below the widget. **Decision:** context renders as one panel of plain prose in both question shapes, items in posted order, no card and no kind label per item, a diagram keeping its comment and expand controls; a prose item past about a dozen lines, in context or at top level, folds under a fade with a Show more that opens it in place, decided by a count of its source; a code excerpt keeps its own scroll box wherever it sits, and a diagram or a mock never folds. **Consequences:** a lone document folds too (one rule, no exception); the sample board and the checks that asserted the card markup regenerate.
