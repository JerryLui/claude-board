# 158. Away: the owner's devices reach the board through Tailscale's forwarder, each browser signed in by a link

2026-11-24, narrows 118; relates to 55, 125, 142, 159

**Context:** the owner reads and answers rounds from a phone and from other Macs; 118's SSH tunnel does not reach a phone, and the daemon answered loopback names alone. Rejected: listening on the network; letting a device in on the login header Tailscale's forwarder adds, which any local program can write, so it needed a key in the forwarder and a refusal of the Mac's own address; a pairing code; naming which devices may connect; merge and Fire answers only at the Mac; not counting an away tab as a Watcher. **Decision:** the daemon still binds `127.0.0.1` only; `tailscale serve` forwards https to it, and besides its loopback names it answers exactly one, the Tailscale name in an owner-only file beside the secret (`src/away.mjs`), which `bin/away.mjs` writes and removes; with no file it answers the names it answered before. The `Host` says which origin a request came in on; no forwarded header grants anything, and one (`X-Forwarded-For`, `-Host` or `-Proto`) refuses a loopback name, since the forwarder passes on the `Host` a device sends; an away request has a signed-in browser's powers and no more: the sign-in's cookies (159) are its only credentials, the secret header is ignored, and the routes open to it are `/auth/<token>` and the mermaid engine a mock's sealed frame loads. A browser is signed in by a link made on the Mac, good for one use and 10 minutes, whose page spends nothing until its button is pressed, since a chat's preview fetches a link nobody tapped. "Do not expose the port" becomes: the port is still never exposed; one name reaches it, inside the owner's Tailscale network, and only a browser signed in by a link gets past the gate. Funnel stays out: `on` refuses while it is on, and the board has no public mode. **Consequences:** 118's tunnel stays, unchanged, for a Mac that shares no Tailscale network. Accepted: every device on the owner's Tailscale login may connect and, once signed in, answer and merge; whoever holds an unlocked signed-in device can merge; an away tab left open counts for auto-open and banners as any tab does, so a new board raises a banner instead of opening; a board page may stay in a browser's storage; a link's page is shown to whoever holds the link for its 10 minutes; `off` does not void a link already made, which works again if `on` follows inside its 10 minutes; a loopback link an agent typed into text does not work away; the first HTTPS certificate publishes the machine's and the network's name for good.
