# 137. Mission cards are as tall as their text, and Boards is one grouped list

2026-10-29, replaces 135; amends 124; relates to 123, 125, 133; narrowed by 142

**Context:** 135 gave every mission card the height of the tallest possible text, so a card with a short summary stood mostly empty, and with its roomy type, padding and a row of always-shown buttons the Missions view read far looser than a list; the Boards view was a grid of cards from 1440px and a card per line below it, so the boards waiting on the person were scattered among the rest. Rejected: keeping that card and dropping only the forced height (it keeps the loose type and padding), Open board and Stop always shown on every card, one ungrouped dense list by time, and the list as it was before the grid. **Decision:** a mission card, and a tab card, is as tall as its text: its title, summary and waiting on stop at 2 lines and next at 1, each ending in an ellipsis, under one Show the rest fold that takes no room while nothing is cut; the card is padded 10 by 12 with 8px corners and no shadow, its title 13px medium under a 12px grey line holding its key and phase (the phase alone when the title is the key). The whole card is the link to its board, the round waiting on the person or else the latest board: its title is a real link (`a.mission-link`) whose box stretches over the card under its chips, buttons, fold and forms, so a click anywhere else on the card, Enter on the link, a middle-click or a Cmd-click opens it as any link does, and with Comment on that press opens the card's note instead. The key line stays under the link's box, so a press on its age, login or folder is the link's own; the card's one tooltip, on the link (or on the card when it has no board), holds the whole title and the key line in full: a mission's exact update time, a tab's login path and folder. Lifting the key line's spans over the link instead was rejected: Stop takes the age's place on hover, and a press re-sent from a span by script loses the browser's own modifier and menu handling. It carries no Open board button. Stop follows the text, so the Tab after the link reaches it; it sits over the card's age and shows on hover, keyboard focus, while its question is open, after Keep or Escape hands it the focus, and always on a screen with no hover; a tab card's key line keeps clear of it; Fire and Merge stay shown, since they are calls, not navigation. Boards is one list in one panel at every width, a 38px line per board (its dot, the title cut with an ellipsis, the rounds-left chip, folder, round count, last update and thread id), the whole line the link, grouped "Waiting on you" (a board with an Awaited round still open, the dot's own fact) over "Earlier", each group headed by its count and left out while empty. **Consequences:** a card's text cannot be selected by a drag, since the link covers it; a repaint that replaces a focused card hands the focus back to its link or Stop; a tab card with several rounds waiting opens the first, the rest reached through Boards; cards in a row no longer line up; a board moves group on the next repaint after its round is answered or posted.
