# 142. A card's Merge opens the brief at its merge question, and answers nothing

2026-10-30, narrows 125, 137; relates to 134

**Context:** a card's Merge answered the landing's merge round from the card (125), so a merge could go through on a card's two lines with the brief behind it unread. Rejected: keeping the one-click merge, tied to the brief's commit, the fastest path but a merge on an unread brief. **Decision:** Merge is a link to `/b/<board>#question:<round>:<question id>` of the action's round and question; the round page resolves that sentinel after load and on `hashchange` by flipping to the round and putting the question's top on screen with the focus (the questions-left pill's landing), and the merge is answered there like any other answer. A colon-bearing sentinel, since no id the page emits is safe to navigate by and no heading slug holds a colon. While a merge waits, the card's summary is the brief's digest and shows whole, up to its 280 characters, rather than clamped at two lines (137). Fire is unchanged: its click still answers its round. **Consequences:** a merge always costs the PM a page open; a stale fragment (a round sent since, a question gone) lands on the board where it opens and does nothing more; a merge-waiting card is as tall as its digest.
