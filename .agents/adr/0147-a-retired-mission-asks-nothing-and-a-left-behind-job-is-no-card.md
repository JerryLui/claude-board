# 147. A retired mission asks nothing and leaves the lists once its work is done; a blocked job its mission moved on from is no card

2026-11-04, amends 131, 140; relates to 123, 128, 141

**Context:** `retiredAt` had one reader, the 30-day purge, so a retired mission whose session had gone read stalled, or needs you while a linked round still asked, under the `waitingOn` it last wrote, and the Master Chief raised it as a live ask. Beside it, a blocked background job whose session had left a released mission was a tab card again (141), in Needs you, though nothing waited on its answer. **Decision:** a mission whose `retiredAt` parses carries `retired: true` on its view and reads `waiting`, never needs you or stalled and never `gone`, whatever its rounds, its session or its `waitingOn`; a released one still reads released. While it has open work, a PR on it GitHub has not read merged (no fact yet counts as open) or a `next` starting `release:`, its card stays under Waiting, tagged Retired; with none it leaves every list, `GET /api/missions` with `all=1` and the full list the missions watch reads included, while a read by key still answers it, and the purge still deletes it 30 days on. The rule is read in the view, so every list follows it. A blocked background job with no process is no tab card once a mission record names its id (or the id its job id is the first 8 characters of) as its session or its ended session, left or not, and that mission is released or another id or a launch's reservation now holds it. Rejected: hiding every retired mission, since a raw write retiring one with an open PR would hide it; reading a stale `waitingOn` as open work, which was the bug; a job settled by Claude Code's idle retire, which fires on jobs still at a live prompt; tying a job to a mission by the mission named at its launch. **Consequences:** a retired mission's Fire or Merge still shows while its round asks, under Waiting; a left session's job on an unreleased mission no one holds keeps its card; moving a settled job's record out of `claude agents` is the session watch's, outside the board.
