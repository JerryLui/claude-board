# 102. One copy of the mechanism, not one caller-facing file

2026-08-12 · narrows 5 and 11

**Context:** entry 11 counted caller-facing *files* and got one, but what it was defending was one copy of the call, the block kinds and the packet — six copies of those had already drifted 148 lines apart. The count and the invariant came apart the moment a caller was worth shipping as a worked example (`skills/grill-example/SKILL.md`) and `INSTALL.md` had to say how a reader routes the callers already on their machine to the board. **Decision:** the repo ships callers and caller procedures freely on one condition — none of them restates mechanism; each names the board and defers to the manual — while `install.sh` goes on installing exactly one file, the manual. An example caller is copied by hand, and the `INSTALL.md` migration inventories read-only, puts a round of questions to the reader, and writes nothing until they say go. **Consequences:** the drift surface stays one file wide however many callers ship, and entry 5's "no callers" narrows to no *installed* callers. Nothing mechanical enforces the pointer rule on a caller shipped from here — `test/check-skill-prose.mjs` binds the manual alone — so a second copy of the mechanism can now land in this repo and pass.
